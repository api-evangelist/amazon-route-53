This case study describes how Uplynk implemented automated multi-Region failover for video streaming infrastructure using Route 53's geolocation and weighted routing policies. The architecture provides resilience across network, application, and data layers including video ingest pipelines, playback services with ElastiCache and MongoDB Atlas, and multi-Region S3 storage, achieving 99.99% availability while supporting 40-60 million hours of video per month.
