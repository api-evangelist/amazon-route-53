This post introduces fine-grained IAM permissions for Route 53 Profiles, enabling organizations to delegate DNS operations to specific teams through condition keys that scope access by resource type, ARN, domain name, and VPC ID. Three real-world scenarios for application teams, security teams, and network engineers are covered with reusable policy templates. The approach allows teams to manage only their designated resources while maintaining centralized DNS governance.
